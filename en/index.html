What it translates

Games

Dialogue, subtitles and menus. The program recognises each game and remembers it; it also works in the PlayStation Remote Play window.

YouTube

With the free Chrome extension, subtitles are translated in sync with the video: while seeking, paused, in full screen.

Windows apps

Menus and buttons in a foreign language show their translation on top. One switch in the panel.

Read from screen

Press Ctrl + F6 and draw a box: text in an image, PDF or photo is translated instantly.

Your privacy stays with you

  • Text recognition runs on your computer; your screenshots are never sent anywhere.
  • With "Local model only", translation runs on your computer too and works with the internet off.
  • Messaging apps, notifications and the lock screen are never read.
  • Text of regular apps and "Read from screen" never goes online and is never written to disk.
