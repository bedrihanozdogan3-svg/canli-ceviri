OverText (Android) — Privacy policy

Developer: Uygun Yazılım · App ID: com.uygunyazilim.overtext
Contact and data deletion requests: bedrihanozdogan3@gmail.com
Last updated: 11 November 2026

OverText reads the text on your phone screen and puts its translation in place of the original. Screen reading and translation happen only on the phone; screenshots and the text read are never sent anywhere.

Disclosure and permission

  • On first launch the app shows a disclosure explaining what is read, what stays on the phone and what goes to the internet. If you do not accept, the screen is not read.
  • If you accept, Android asks for the screen sharing permission; while sharing is on, the phone shows it in the status bar. You can stop it at any time with the Stop button in the notification.

What it reads

  • A screenshot is taken only when you tap the Çevir (Translate) button at the edge of the screen or while Sürekli (Continuous) mode is on. Text is read on the phone; screenshots are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Copy-protected screens (Netflix, banking apps and similar) come through black by the phone's own rule and are not read.
  • In this version the app does not check which app is open: if you tap the button in a messaging app, or Continuous mode is on there, that screen is read and translated too. This also stays on the phone.

Translation happens on the phone

  • English-to-Turkish translation is done on the phone by our own translation model, which comes with the app. Other languages are translated with Google's ML Kit component, also on the phone. No text is sent online.

Internet

  • Only on first use, the language pack and ML Kit's own working settings are downloaded from Google's servers. No screenshot or read text is sent in these requests. Google sees the phone's network address, the technical details the download needs (such as app and component version) and a random identifier ML Kit gives this installation (not meant to identify you or the phone); these fall under Google's own privacy terms.
  • Usage statistics are off: ML Kit normally sends Google anonymous usage statistics (device model, app name, duration, language; not the read text). This sending is switched off in OverText.
  • There are no ads, analytics or tracking tools. No account is created and no personal information is asked for.

What stays on the phone

Everything below is in the app's own folder on the phone, which other apps cannot access.

  • Downloaded language packs and your chosen target language.
  • Translation memory: texts read and their translations, so the same text is not translated again. At most 5,000 entries are kept; the oldest is deleted.
  • Screen memory: kept so that when you return to a screen you have seen (menu, inventory, map) the translation appears at once. It holds a coarse grey trace of that screen (one 16-level brightness value for each 40 × 40-pixel patch; not an image, text cannot be read), the texts read on it, their positions and translations. At most 300 screens are kept; the least used is deleted.
  • The translation memory and screen memory are written to a file when the app closes and read when it opens. Screenshots themselves are never written to disk.
  • The app's own log holds only numbers and status (such as how many texts were read; never the read text). When it passes 1 MB, the previous log is deleted.
  • Google's translation component writes the sentence it translates to the phone's own system log. That log stays on the phone and OverText never sends it anywhere.
  • None of this leaves the phone: OverText sends none of these files anywhere. They are also excluded from Android's cloud backup and from transfer to a new phone; both are switched off in the app.

Deleting your data

  • Go to the phone's Settings → Apps → OverText → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Either one deletes all of the above.
  • We hold no data about you. For questions and requests: bedrihanozdogan3@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
